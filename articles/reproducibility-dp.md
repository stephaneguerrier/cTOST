# Equivalence Testing under Privacy Constraints (2026)

This page will host the code that reproduces the results of

> Pareek, S., Insolia, L., Molinari, R. & Guerrier, S. (2026).
> *Equivalence testing under privacy constraints*. arXiv preprint
> [arXiv:2604.06499](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.06499).

The methods of this paper are available in the package through
[`tost_equiv_dp()`](https://stephaneguerrier.github.io/cTOST/reference/tost_equiv_dp.md)
and
[`prop_test_equiv_dp()`](https://stephaneguerrier.github.io/cTOST/reference/prop_test_equiv_dp.md);
see the [Differential Privacy Equivalence
Testing](https://stephaneguerrier.github.io/cTOST/articles/dp-equivalence.md)
article for their use.
